# Financial Indicators

## Valuation And Income Statement

- Default valuation check: `Market Cap / Annual Net Income < 30`.
- Show market cap, annual net income, and the division used.
- Show Revenue, Gross Profit, and Net Income by year with year-over-year change.

## Balance Sheet

- `Total Assets > Total Liabilities`.
- Risk-bearing check: `Total Liabilities / Net Income < 4`.
- If net income is zero or negative, do not present the ratio as a normal
  positive-risk multiple; explain why it is not meaningful.

## Cash Flow

- Compare Operating Cash Flow with Investing Cash Flow using clearly stated
  sign treatment.
- Show Free Cash Flow by year.
- Check `Free Cash Flow / Income < 30%`, while explicitly naming which income
  measure is used. If the original intent is ambiguous, report the calculated
  value and ask before treating it as an investment rule.

## Other

- Include shares outstanding when available.
- Use annual series with at least three years and prefer five.
- Mark pass with `✅`, fail with `❌`, and unavailable with `—`.
